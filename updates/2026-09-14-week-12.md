# EPF Week 12 - Ivan Anishchuk - pure-path Merkle results on main

Short version:

- Leo's [#100](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/100) put the pure-path Merkle
  results on main, including both halves of branch completeness. It builds on the branch
  module from my [#71](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/71).
- My old stack is closed: branch completeness
  ([#42](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/42)), generalized indices
  ([#66](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/66)), the `hash_tree_root` agreement
  ([#67](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/67)) and perfect combine-trees
  ([#72](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/72)). What it still adds is in two draft
  PRs, [#111](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/111) and
  [#112](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/112).

## What landed

[#100](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/100) proves merkleization, generalized
indices and branch completeness on the pure, uncached path, and puts the cached tree out of
proof scope (it stays the execution path, checked by the conformance sweep). My old stack
was stated over the cached tree, so most of it is now either covered by Leo's PR or out of
scope.

The [etheorem update on ethresear.ch](https://ethresear.ch/t/etheorem-update-the-complete-executable-consensus-specs-written-in-lean-4/26063)
has the current state in its appendices: `isValidMerkleBranch_iff` in Appendix A, and in
Appendix B what's proved on merkleization (path bits, openings, the gindex model, branch
completeness) and what's only tested (the cached tree's update path).

## What's still outstanding

[#100](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/100) left branch completeness in two
halves: one theorem takes "the opening folds back to the root" as a hypothesis, another
proves it. [#111](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/111) composes them, so
`is_valid_merkle_branch` accepts the opening `merkleize` itself builds, for a chunk list,
a depth and an index. It also adds two tests for the length guard: one branch has a
sibling too many, the other a sibling too few.

[#112](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/112) builds on it. A branch to a container
field verifies against the container's `hash_tree_root`, at one, two and three levels of
nesting, each with a version stated over the generalized index. Tests check the spec's
gindex constants against our `generalizedIndex` on the Fulu and Gloas schemas, and the
theorems apply at Fulu's
`finalized_checkpoint.root` (169) and at the Gloas body's execution block hash
(`EXECUTION_BLOCK_HASH_GINDEX_GLOAS` = 832, three levels down through the payload bid).

The proofs use one axiom: that the FFI SHA-256 combine (the 32-byte case) matches the spec.
With the pure-Lean hasher they use none.

Two things are still not covered: the deposit contract's incremental tree and the light
client's normalized branch form. Both need modeling before completeness can even be stated.
