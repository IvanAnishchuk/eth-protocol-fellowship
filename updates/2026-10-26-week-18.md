## Casper FFG

[#139](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/139) has an abstract FFG model in which each side
of a fork keeps its own stake, a proof that the spec's justification and finalization step refines
that model, and the theorem for two honest fork-choice stores. If one store's justified checkpoint
conflicts with the other's finalized checkpoint, the blocks they've seen contain slashable
attestation pairs. The validators who signed both attestations of a pair hold at least a third of
the stake, minus a term for how far the stake drifted after the fork. The PR also bounds that
drift. A test builds two conflicting chains at the minimal preset and checks that
`process_attester_slashing` accepts the evidence.

## Finality for the censored payload

The week 16 race assumed a fixed justified checkpoint no later than the block.
[#140](https://github.com/etheorem/etheorem/pull/140) drops that: the race runs over several epochs,
and once the justified checkpoint moves past the block, it is on the EMPTY side. If the honest
votes are included in time, every honest node finalizes on the EMPTY side within four epochs.
After that, a head with the payload requires slashing evidence, by the FFG theorem. A two-node test
run at the minimal preset meets every premise and finalizes epoch 4 at slot 64.
